Privacy Policy

Effective September 12, 2026
The short version: MetaClean scans and cleans photo metadata entirely on your device. We don't have a server, we don't have accounts, and we never see, receive, or store your photos. Two things that do touch the network are disclosed in full below: the optional map preview, and the ad service that supports the free version of the app.
No photo ever uploaded
No account or sign-in
Nothing sold to third parties

1. What MetaClean does

MetaClean reads and removes hidden metadata from photos you choose on your own device — EXIF, XMP, IPTC/Photoshop fields, and C2PA content-credential data. All scanning and cleaning happens locally, using the device's own processor and storage. A photo you open in MetaClean is never transmitted anywhere as part of that process.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not operate a server that receives your photos, and we do not collect:

  • Your photos, or any metadata extracted from them (GPS, camera info, timestamps, authorship, or AI-provenance data)
  • Your name, email address, or any account credentials — MetaClean has no accounts
  • Payment information — MetaClean has no in-app purchases

3. Information stored on your device

The following stays on your device, under your operating system's normal app storage, and is never transmitted by MetaClean:

Cleaning historyFile names, timestamps, and counts of data points removed, so you can review past cleans. Clearable anytime from the History screen.

Cleaned photo copiesOptionally saved to your device's photo library when you choose to save or share a result.

4. Third-party services

Two features rely on third-party services. Both are disclosed here in full.

4.1 Map preview (OpenStreetMap)

If a photo has GPS coordinates, the Inspect screen can show its location on a map. This preview loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap's tile servers. Like any map service, that request includes the coordinates being viewed and standard network request metadata (such as your IP address). This only happens when you view a location found in a photo you opened — it is not triggered otherwise, and no other photo metadata is sent with it.

4.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of MetaClean shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers, approximate location, and other information to serve and measure ads, subject to your device's ad-tracking and privacy settings. This data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not by MetaClean directly. No photo or photo metadata is ever shared with the ad network.
